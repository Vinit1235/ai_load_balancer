Frequently Asked Questions
How do I get started with GetConnect?

Sign up for a free account, generate your API key from the dashboard, and install our SDK with npm install @getconnect/sdk. You can submit your first task in under 5 minutes.

What happens if a node fails during task execution?

Our AI load balancer automatically detects node failures and redistributes the task to a healthy node within milliseconds. You'll receive a webhook notification and the task will complete without interruption.

How is billing calculated?

Billing is based on compute time (CPU-seconds) and the number of tasks executed per month. Free tier includes 10,000 tasks/month. Pro and Enterprise plans offer higher limits with volume discounts.

Can I use GetConnect with my existing infrastructure?

Yes. GetConnect integrates with AWS, GCP, Azure, and on-premise servers. Use our REST API or SDKs (JS, Python, Java, Go) to connect your existing systems in minutes.

What SLA do you offer?

We guarantee 99.9% uptime on Pro plans and 99.99% on Enterprise. SLA credits are automatically applied if we fall below these thresholds.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your account settings under Billing. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. No cancellation fees.
