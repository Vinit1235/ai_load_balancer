Frequently Asked Questions

What are your support hours?

+

Our technical support team is available 24/7 for critical issues. Standard support hours are Monday-Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM in your local timezone. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 dedicated support.

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

+

Response times vary by priority: Critical issues (under 1 hour), High priority (under 4 hours), Normal priority (under 24 hours), Low priority (within 48 hours). Enterprise SLA guarantees faster response times.

Do you offer phone support?

+

Phone support is available for Pro and Enterprise plan customers. Free tier users can access support through email and our community forums. Contact information for phone support is provided in your account dashboard.

Can I schedule a technical consultation?

+

Yes! We offer free technical consultations for all plans. You can schedule a 30-minute session with our technical team through your dashboard or by contacting sales. Enterprise customers have access to dedicated technical account managers.

Where can I find documentation and tutorials?

+

Complete documentation, API references, and video tutorials are available in our Documentation section. We also regularly publish tutorials and guides on our Blog.
