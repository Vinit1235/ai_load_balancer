Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and plans

Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to the new features and be billed the prorated difference. When downgrading, changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial available?
Yes! All new users get a 14-day free trial of our Professional plan. No credit card required to start. After the trial, you can choose any plan that fits your needs.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. Enterprise customers can also be invoiced.
How does the AI load balancing work?
Our AI analyzes task complexity, node availability, network latency, and historical performance to distribute tasks optimally. It continuously learns from system behavior to improve distribution efficiency over time.
What happens if I exceed my monthly task limit?
We'll notify you when you reach 80% and 100% of your monthly limit. Once exceeded, additional tasks are billed at $0.0005 per task (or you can upgrade your plan). You can set hard limits in your account settings.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or educational institutions?
Yes! We offer special pricing for qualified non-profit organizations, educational institutions, and research projects. Contact our sales team with documentation of your status to learn more.
